# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). The full text there applies; this page summarises it.

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, colour, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and kind, including to people who have fallen for scams. Being targeted is never someone's fault.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the people Satark protects.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks.
- Publishing anyone's private information (names, phone numbers, account or UPI details) without explicit permission. This includes pasting real scam messages that identify victims.
- Sharing working scam kits, phishing pages, or techniques intended to defraud people.
- Any other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainer, [@Ankur17nith](https://github.com/Ankur17nith), through a private channel (for example GitHub's [private vulnerability reporting](https://github.com/Ankur17nith/satark/security/advisories/new) form, marked "Code of Conduct"). All reports will be reviewed promptly and kept confidential. The maintainer may remove, edit or reject contributions, and temporarily or permanently ban contributors, for behaviour that violates this code.
