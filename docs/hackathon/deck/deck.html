Satark02
Why people fall for it

Scams run on pressure,
not technology

Fear

"Arrest warrant in your name." "Account blocked today."

Urgency

"Within 2 hours." "Electricity cut tonight."

Authority

Fake CBI, police, RBI, TRAI and bank officers.

Isolation

"Stay on the video call. Don't tell your family."

Greed

"Earn ₹3,000 a day for liking videos."

The gap: awareness campaigns explain scams in general, but nothing helps at the moment someone is holding a specific, frightening message, alone and in a hurry.
